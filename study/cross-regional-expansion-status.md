# Cross-regional expansion status

**Updated: 2026-10-09**

## Scope

This expansion adds India, Indonesia, Ecuador, and Malawi to the original eight regional units: mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, the United Kingdom, and the United States. The source index currently contains 44 candidate series records across 12 jurisdiction units. It is a discovery and documentation index, not an exhaustive textbook census or a record of national adoption.

EF EPI 2025 is used only as a purposive case-selection reference for India and three countries with scores near China. Its test-taker population is adult and self-selected; the index is not a school-quality measure or a pupil outcome.

## Evidence status

Officially hosted or publisher-linked student-book pages provide a small number of traceable examples of requests, information-seeking questions, classroom clarification, and a possible turn-entry expression. These examples show why grammatical form alone cannot determine pragmatic function. They do not support comparable country-level frequencies, school adoption rates, or learner-effect claims.

Page-level candidate quotations and annotation records remain local and are not included in this public source map. The current candidate ledger has not undergone independent double coding and adjudication; no cross-regional frequency estimates are reported.

## Access and reuse

The public files contain source metadata, public links, and this status note only. They do not contain textbook chapters, page images, event quotations, learner records, or annotations. Public accessibility, a government-hosted PDF, or a free sample does not by itself establish an open licence. Copyright and reuse terms should be checked for each edition before extraction, quotation, storage, or redistribution.
