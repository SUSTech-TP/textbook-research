# International school English textbook source map

This directory records candidate textbook series and official or publisher source links for cross-regional English textbook research. It is a source map, not a census of all titles or a record of every school's current adoption.

## Scope and interpretation

- The series index records bibliographic metadata, access and licence status, and whether a target-form signal has been verified at page level. “Not yet verified” is not evidence that a form is absent.
- It does not include textbook chapters, page images, full-text transcriptions, student records, or annotation datasets.
- The EF EPI 2025 sheet is used only as adult English-proficiency context for purposive case selection. Its scores are not school quality ratings or pupil proficiency estimates.
- Local curriculum, textbook adoption, grade mapping, and language-of-instruction systems remain distinct; do not infer national adoption from a publisher's catalogue.

## Files

- `manifest.csv`: textbook-series source metadata and access/licence notes.
- `EF_EPI_2025_selection.csv`: country scores, ranks, and case-selection context with official source URLs.
